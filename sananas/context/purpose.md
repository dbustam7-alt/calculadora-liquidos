# Project Purpose: DDMRP - Perfiles Cobertura

## Vision
Transform theoretical DDMRP protection factors into an operational consulting framework that simplifies inventory decision-making through "Days of Coverage" metrics.

## Core Problem Solved
DDMRP standard formulas can be abstract for stakeholders. By translating Lead Time and Variability factors into specific "Red Zone Days of Coverage," we provide a clear, intuitive signal for inventory protection.

## Unique Selling Proposition
Statistical segmentation of SKUs based on both Variability (CV) and Continuity (ADi), allowing for more robust protection in intermittent demand scenarios.

## Target Audience
Supply Chain Managers, Inventory Analysts, and Antigravity Consultants.

## Deployment Strategy
Calculations performed in Python/Excel, with results presented in a standardized summary table for consulting engagements.

## Commercial Success
Higher inventory stability and reduced stockouts/overstock by aligning buffer protection with actual consumption patterns.
